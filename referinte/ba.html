    The Curriculum

    Every Topic in Personal Branding

    Covered across 3 days. No filler — just the frameworks that actually build brands.

    01Brand Positioning
    How you occupy space in your audience's mind — the wedge that makes you the only logical choice.
    02Content Messaging
    The exact language, angles and hooks that make your message land and stick with the people you want to reach.
    03Viral Ideation
    A repeatable system for generating content ideas engineered to spread — without chasing trends or luck.
    04Cinematics & Perception
    How visual quality shapes the perceived value of your brand — and the production choices that make you look like the category leader.
    05Value Content
    Frameworks for teaching and giving so much value that your audience trusts you before they ever buy.
    06Storytelling Frameworks
    The structures behind content that makes people feel something, remember you, and take action.
    07Distribution at Scale
    How to put your content in front of the right people, on every platform, with a system that compounds over time.
